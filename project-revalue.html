ReValue – AI Waste Bank

ReValue is an AI-powered digital waste bank ecosystem designed to modernize conventional recycling workflows. Manual waste sorting and valuation are often time-consuming and inconsistent. ReValue addresses this challenge by integrating Computer Vision to automatically classify non-organic waste, estimate item weight, and calculate real-time economic value directly through a user-friendly mobile interface.

Smart Waste Classification

Automatically identifies non-organic waste categories (plastic, metal, paper, glass) from camera input.

Weight & Value Estimation

Predicts item mass and converts it into transparent, real-time market exchange value.

Digital Eco-Wallet

Tracks user recycling contributions, environmental impact, and redeemable financial balances.
