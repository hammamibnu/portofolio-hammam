ReValue – AI Waste Bank

ReValue is an AI-powered digital waste bank platform designed to support the circular economy by helping users identify, weigh, and monetize non-organic household waste. Using Computer Vision recognition, the app automatically classifies waste materials, calculates net economic value after pickup fees, and credits earnings directly into the user's digital wallet.

AI Waste Recognition

Classifies non-organic waste categories (Plastic, Paper & Cardboard, Metal, Glass) with real-time confidence scoring.

Transparent Value & Pickup

Calculates gross value, pickup fees, and net earnings while offering individual sale or community pooling options.

Impact Tracking & ReValue Wallet

Monitors total recycled weight, transaction verification status, and redeemable wallet balances.

Interface & System Showcase

1. Home Screen & Daily Impact

Main dashboard featuring quick access to AI waste scanning, daily earnings summary, recycled weight metrics, and current material rates (Plastic, Paper & Cardboard, Metal).

2. Impact Analytics & Transaction Log

Tracks environmental impact over customizable timeframes (Today, 7 Days, 30 Days) alongside real-time verification status for each waste deposit.

3. Real-Time AI Waste Scanner

Camera capture interface equipped with framing guides and best-practice tips to ensure accurate lighting and single-item classification.

4. AI Recognition & Action Options

Displays detected category (Paper & Cardboard) with an 87.3% confidence score, estimated weight, net value, and options to sell directly or pool with other users.

5. Valuation Breakdown & Pickup Request

Transparent calculation of gross value, pickup service fee, and net payout, complete with automated transaction ID generation and courier pickup scheduling.

6. ReValue Digital Wallet

Dedicated wallet screen displaying accumulated balance from verified recycling payouts and itemized disbursement history by transaction ID.
