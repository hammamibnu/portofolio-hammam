Smart Campus Crowdsourcing & Indoor Navigation

Internet of Things & Indoor Positioning | Academic Project (TEMU Platform)

An academic smart campus platform designed to help students and visitors navigate indoor environments, locate campus facilities, and report or reclaim lost items collaboratively. The system combines BLE Beacon and Wi-Fi indoor positioning concepts with a WebSocket-powered crowdsourcing web application (TEMU) for real-time campus utility updates.

Campus Indoor Navigation

Designed an indoor navigation system concept to help students and visitors locate campus facilities and incident rooms accurately.

BLE & Wi-Fi Positioning

Integrated Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Beacon and Wi-Fi-based positioning approaches to support indoor location estimation where GPS signals are limited.

WebSocket Crowdsourcing

Designed a WebSocket-based crowdsourcing concept enabling real-time sharing of campus facility availability and lost-and-found reports.

Platform Prototype & Crowdsourcing Interface

1. Campus Crowdsourcing Feed

Main dashboard enabling students to search and filter real-time campus reports by keyword, category, status, and indoor building location.

2. Location-Tagged Report Submission

Structured submission form allowing users to log item details, indoor floor/room locations, reporter contacts, and supporting photos.

3. Personal Report Tracking

User dashboard to monitor active submissions, review incoming claims, and update case completion status in real time.

4. Administrative Moderation Panel

Centralized oversight table for campus administrators to verify reports, track claim counts, and manage system records.
